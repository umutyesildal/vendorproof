The Problem

AI research tools invent citations.

  • A URL in a report ≠ the claim is true
  • Vendor due diligence needs receipts, not vibes
  • Manual verification takes hours per vendor
Everyone here has clicked a citation that didn't say what the AI claimed. In vendor due diligence that's not annoying — it's a bad procurement decision. Objection prep: "Isn't this just RAG?" → RAG retrieves text; it doesn't verify the claim against it. We gate on proof.
The Solution

Every claim carries its evidence —
or says it can't.

PLANS

Self-directed research

The model decomposes the objective into its own queries — not a fixed script.

VERIFIES

Deterministic validator

A citation only counts if its verbatim quote exists inside the cited source. Downgrades only — never upgrades.

ACTS

Real automation

Dedupe, job tracking, risk alerts, failure recovery — running on n8n right now.

The middle card is the moat. The LLM proposes; deterministic code disposes. "insufficient_evidence" is a first-class honest result — that honesty is the product.
